## Full description
FINPA is your Financial Personal Assistant for everyday money tracking.

Log expenses in plain language, review your monthly budget, keep a clean ledger, and export your records when you need them. FINPA is built for real daily spending: transport, food, salary, subscriptions, family support, side-hustle expenses, and cash transactions.

Key features:
- Chat expense logging: write natural entries like “Spent ₦2,500 on fuel”.
- Manual entry: add income and expenses even when you prefer forms.
- Voice capture: speak expenses on supported Android builds.
- Budget vs actuals: compare planned spending against real spending.
- Clean ledger: review, edit, and organize monthly transactions.
- CSV export: download statements for Excel or Google Sheets.
- PIN activation: buy access online and activate securely in the app.

FINPA helps you stop guessing where your money went and start seeing your spending clearly.
